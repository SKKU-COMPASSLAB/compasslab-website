### We won the Best Paper Award at ITC-CSCC 2025!

The paper “Outlier Matters: A Statistical Analysis of LLM Tensor Distributions and Quantization Effects” received the Best Paper Award at the 40th International Technical Conference on Circuits/Systems, Computers and Communications (ITC-CSCC 2025). Congratulations to Taein Kim, Seongwook Kim, Sukhyun Han, Woojin Cho, Youngjae Choi, Youngseok Bae, and Seokin Hong!
